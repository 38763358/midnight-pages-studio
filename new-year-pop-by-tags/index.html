What you can edit

The listing’s edit steps are: open the PPTX, type your name, phone, email, and brokerage, and place a headshot in the round photo slot. The greeting is the design, not a blank line.

Canva use, if you want it, is an import of that PPTX. It is not a shared Canva template you copy with one click, and it does not require a Canva Pro seat to be described honestly: the file itself is the PPTX.

How to print

  1. Export the edited hang tag as a PDF.
  2. Print at 100% on matte cardstock. A “fit to page” setting will change the 2.75 × 4 inch trim, and then it will not match the bottleneck hole you planned.
  3. Trim, punch, and tie twine long enough to loop the neck of the bottle.
