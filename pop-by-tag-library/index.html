What the 115 counts, and what it leaves out

The number on the cover is a tag count, and it is easy to misread. Here is the addition the listing itself uses.

  • 109 collection designs across eight sets: Chalk Talk 14, Curb Appeal 13, Fair Isle Lodge 13, Gilded Hush 14, Gingham Kitchen 14, Herbarium Market 13, Postmark Society 14, Riso Papercut 14. Those add to 109.

Occasions you can actually cover

The eight collections include Thanksgiving, Christmas and holiday, New Year, Valentine’s Day, St. Patrick’s Day, spring, home-iversary, housewarming, and anytime thank-you tags. That is the range the listing names. A collection tag is the right tool for “thank you for the referral” or “happy closing anniversary” when you want a style (gingham, chalk, botanical) rather than a single illustrated gift.

The six seasonal signature tags are the illustrated gift tags:

Mother’s Day, summer, the Fourth of July, August, fall, and Halloween are in the $9 twelve-month bundle. They are not in this list of six. Buying the Library does not, on the published contents, include those six months.

How to print

  1. Edit the PPTX (or the newsletter DOCX) and export a PDF.
  2. Print tags at 100% on matte cardstock. The collection sheets are US Letter with crop marks. Seasonal squares are 3.5 × 3.5 inches; the New Year tag in this Library is the 2.75 × 4 inch hang tag.
  3. Trim, punch, and tie the tag to a gift you bought. The newsletters are for sending or printing as letters, not for the gift bag.

Questions

Is 115 the number of files, or the number of tag designs?

115 is the tag-design count published on the listing: 109 designs in the eight collections, plus 6 seasonal signature tags. The 12 newsletters are extra Word files and are not part of the 115. Chalk Talk’s 14 designs each also come in a chalkboard version and a white ink-saver version; those versions are not added on top of the 14, and not on top of the 115.

Does this include the $9 twelve-month bundle?

It includes six seasonal tags: Thanksgiving, Christmas, New Year, Valentine’s Day, St. Patrick’s Day, and Spring. It does not list the bundle’s May through October tags (Mother’s Day, summer, Fourth of July, August, fall, Halloween). Those six months are in the $9 twelve-month product. The newsletters in this Library are a third thing: 12 letters, not 12 tags.

Is the New Year tag in here the hang tag or the square?

The Library listing says the New Year seasonal is a 2.75 × 4 inch hang tag, and the other five seasonal tags in the Library are 3.5 × 3.5 inch squares. The $9 bundle instead lists January, including that same “new keys” line, as a 3.5 × 3.5 inch square. Do not mix those sizes up.

What can I edit?

On the eight collections, the listing says you can edit your name, brokerage, and phone number, and that the main greeting is part of each design. On the six seasonal signature tags, it says you can edit name, phone, email, and brokerage, and add a headshot in a round slot. Those are different edit ranges. Do not assume every one of the 115 has a photo slot.

Is there a one-click Canva template?

No. The listing says no Canva account is required. You can import the six seasonal signature PPTX files into Canva if you prefer. The eight collections are edited from their PPTX files in PowerPoint, Keynote, or Google Slides, and each collection also includes 300 dpi PNGs. None of this is a shared Canva link.

How does the $15 compare with buying pieces?

The eight collections are $3 each, $24. The six seasonal singles are $3 each, $18. The listing calls that $42 of tags for $15, with the newsletters included as well. The $9 twelve-month bundle is a separate product and is not the thing being added up in that $42.
