It does not describe a photo slot on these five tags. If you need a round headshot on a Christmas or New Year tag, that is the seasonal product, not this sampler.

How to print

  1. Print pages 2–6 at 100% / actual size. Turn off “fit to page.” A test sheet on plain paper is worth it.
  2. Use heavy matte cardstock, in the range the listing gives: 65–110 lb.
  3. Cut on the crop marks, punch the hole, sign the “from” line if you are writing it by hand, and tie the tag on.

The wording is meant to stay fair-housing friendly. The license on the listing is your own business marketing. Please don’t resell or share the files.
