Where this tag earns its place

December pop-bys get lost in a stack of cards. A jar of hot cocoa mix is small enough to leave on a porch and specific enough that someone knows it was chosen. The tag is what makes the jar yours: your name, your phone, and a headshot, on a 3.5 inch square that already says “Thanks a choco-lot! For your referrals.”

A practical route is the first two weeks of December, while people are home and not yet traveling. Tie the tag to cocoa mix, a handful of marshmallows, or a mug. One evening of printing and tying covers a past-client loop. It is a thank-you, not a referral fee. Check your brokerage’s gift rules before you treat it as anything else.

The line on the tag mentions Christmas. For clients who do not celebrate Christmas, use a different tag. The free sampler includes a “Merry Everything” design and a winter “Stay cozy” design that are not Christmas-specific.

What you can edit, and how

Type your name, phone, email, and brokerage into the PPTX. Drop your headshot into the round photo slot. The illustration and the cocoa line stay as drawn. That is the whole editable range the listing describes.

If you prefer Canva, import the PPTX. Do not expect a “copy this Canva template” link in the download. PowerPoint, Keynote, and Google Slides open the same PPTX without Canva.

How to print

  1. Export the edited tag as a PDF.
  2. Print at 100% (actual size) on matte cardstock. Turn off “fit to page.”
  3. Trim on the crop marks, punch the hole, and tie the tag to the cocoa with twine.

Run one test on plain paper before you print the stack, so the 3.5 inch square has not been scaled.

Questions

Is a physical cocoa kit included?

No. This is a digital file. You print the tag and attach it to cocoa, marshmallows, or a mug that you buy yourself. Nothing is shipped.

How many designs are in the download?

One tag design. The print PDF includes that same design twice in layout: once as a single tag with bleed and crop marks, and once as four copies on a US Letter sheet. Those are print layouts, not four different designs.

Can I open this in Canva with one click?

No. There is no Canva template link. The editable file is a PPTX. Open it in PowerPoint, Keynote, or Google Slides, or import that PPTX into Canva.

What can I change?

The listing says you type your name, phone, email, and brokerage, and you add a headshot in the round photo slot. The illustration and the “Thanks a choco-lot” line are the design.

What size does it print?

3.5 × 3.5 inches, plus 0.125 inch bleed. Print at 100% on matte cardstock, then trim, punch, and add twine.

Is this the same file as December in the $9 bundle or the $15 Library?

Those are separate Gumroad products. The 12-month bundle lists a December tag with the same line and the same published trim size, 3.5 × 3.5 inches. The Library lists this Christmas tag as one of its six seasonal tags. This $3 listing is only the Christmas tag.
