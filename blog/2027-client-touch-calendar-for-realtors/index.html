The 2027 Client Touch Calendar for Realtors

Four pop-bys, twelve newsletters and a few small touches in between. A year-long plan you can set up in an afternoon.

Midnight Pages Studio · October 19, 2026

Most agents mean to stay in touch with past clients and then get busy. A calendar fixes that. Decide now what goes out each month, and the year mostly runs itself. Here’s a simple rhythm: a newsletter every month, a pop-by roughly once a season, and a personal touch on each client’s own dates.

The basic rhythm

  • Monthly: a short newsletter by email or print. Useful home content, a few local dates, a personal note.
  • Quarterly-ish: a pop-by gift with a tag, for your top past clients and sphere.
  • Personal dates: a card or text on home anniversaries (the closing date) and birthdays if you have them.

Pick a pace you can keep. Four pop-bys a year that actually happen beat twelve that don’t.

Month by month

January
New Year pop-by or note: “Cheers to 2027.” Newsletter: setting home goals for the year, a winter maintenance checklist.
February
Newsletter: a small Valentine’s note to clients, an indoor project idea. Good month to send home anniversary cards you missed.
March
Newsletter: spring cleaning and the change to daylight saving time (March 14, 2027), a reminder to test smoke detectors.
April
Spring pop-by: seed packets, a small plant, or flower bulbs with an anytime tag. Newsletter: yard and exterior prep.
May
Newsletter: garden and outdoor season, a local farmers market list. Pop-by option: a small potted herb.
June
Newsletter: summer home checklist, AC filters, a note for anyone moving this summer.
July
Summer pop-by around the Fourth: popsicles, a flag, or a lemonade mix with an anytime tag. Newsletter: summer events in your area.
August
Newsletter: back-to-school routines, getting the house ready for fall.
September
Newsletter: fall maintenance, gutters and heating check. Start planning your fall pop-bys.
October
Fall pop-by: a small pumpkin or caramel apples. Newsletter: Halloween safety, end-of-year planning.
November
Thanksgiving pop-by the week before the holiday (Thursday, November 25, 2027). Newsletter: gratitude and hosting tips. See Thanksgiving pop-by ideas.
December
Holiday pop-by in the first two weeks. Newsletter: holiday events, a year-end thank-you. See December pop-by ideas.

What goes in a client newsletter

A good client newsletter is useful first and about you second. A simple structure:

  • A short seasonal feature: one practical idea for the home.
  • A homeowner checklist for the month.
  • Local dates to know.
  • A market snapshot using your own MLS numbers. Don’t borrow national stats that don’t reflect your area.
  • A personal note and an easy way to reach you.
