What you can edit

For each month, type your name, phone, email, and brokerage, and add a headshot in the round slot. You do that in the PPTX. The pun and the illustration are the design for that month. Importing the PPTX into Canva is optional. There is no separate Canva link to copy.

The Realtor Template Kit on the homepage ($69) lists this 12-month set as a bonus alongside the Library and a prompt pack. The kit is a different product, aimed at listing presentations and closing documents. This page sells the twelve tags on their own.

How to print a month

  1. Open that month’s PPTX and export a PDF after you add your details.
  2. Print at 100% on matte cardstock.
  3. Trim, punch, and tie the tag to that month’s gift.

Printing all twelve in January is optional. Many agents print the next month only, so a headshot change or a phone change does not waste a year of cardstock.

Questions

Is this the Pop-By Tag Library?

No. This is 12 monthly tags for $9. The Library is 115 tag designs (109 in eight collections, plus 6 seasonal tags) and, separately, 12 newsletters, for $15. Buying this bundle does not give you the eight collections or the newsletters.

Does the Library replace this bundle?

No. The Library’s seasonal tags stop at six: Thanksgiving, Christmas, New Year, Valentine’s Day, St. Patrick’s Day, and Spring. This bundle also has May through October: Mother’s Day, summer, the Fourth of July, August, fall, and Halloween. If you want those six months, they are in this $9 listing, not described as part of the Library.

Is January the same file as the $3 New Year hang tag?

Do not treat them as the same size. This bundle lists January as one of twelve squares at 3.5 × 3.5 inches, with the line “Cheers to new keys in 2027!” The separate New Year product is a 2.75 × 4 inch bottle hang tag with that same line. Same words, different trim size on the two listings.

How many designs, exactly?

Twelve, one per month. Each month has its own PPTX. The print PDF for each month is that one design: a single tag with bleed and crop marks, plus four copies on US Letter.

Can I edit these in Canva?

Import each month’s PPTX into Canva, or edit it in PowerPoint, Keynote, or Google Slides. There is no one-click Canva template link in the download.

Does anything get mailed?

No. The ZIP is the product. You print the tags and you buy the pie, the cocoa, the peaches, and the rest.
